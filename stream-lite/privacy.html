Stream Lite Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 30, 2026

Overview

Stream Lite is a video browser app. The app does not require an account and does not collect personal information directly from users.

Information Stored On Your Device

The app may store your app preferences, theme choice, playback settings, sleep timer settings, and saved shortcuts/favorites locally on your device. This information is used only to make the app work as expected and is not sent to the app developer.

Third-Party Websites

Stream Lite lets you open third-party websites inside a browser view. Those websites may collect information according to their own privacy policies, cookies, account settings, and terms. Stream Lite does not control the privacy practices of third-party websites.

Analytics and Advertising

Stream Lite does not include developer-operated analytics SDKs and does not collect advertising identifiers for tracking.

Support and Bug Reports

If you choose to contact support or send a bug report by email, the app developer will receive the email address you use, the message content you provide, and basic technical details included in the email template, such as the app version and platform. This information is used only to respond to support requests and troubleshoot issues.

Children

Stream Lite is not directed to children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
